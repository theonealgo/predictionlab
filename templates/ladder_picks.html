How the ladder works

  • Day 1 starts with a $10 wager on one moneyline, spread or total pick from our published predictions, priced between -150 and +150.
  • A pick is used only when that kind of pick has won at least 60% of 40 or more graded games against sportsbook lines over the past year, and held up in both halves of that year.
  • A win adds the profit, at the odds recorded when the pick was published, to the next day's wager.
  • A loss ends the ladder. A push or void is not a loss: the wager carries to the next day unchanged.
  • If no pick meets our requirements on a given date, no pick is forced: the ladder waits for the next qualifying pick. Ladder days count picks, not calendar days.
  • A ladder that wins through Day 10 is complete. One ladder runs at a time.
  • Picks never change after they are published.
