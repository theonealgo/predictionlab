Glossary of Terms

Quantitative sports analytics & probability reference index
Expected Value (+EV)

A mathematical measure where the model's projected probability for an outcome exceeds the probability implied by the broader market. Tracking positive expected value quantifies statistical advantage relative to consensus benchmarks.

Outright Win Probability

The statistical likelihood of a team winning a matchup outright, independent of point margins or handicap calculations.

Consensus Model

Prediction Lab's multi-model aggregation system that combines signals from five independent machine-learning models into a single ensemble forecast.

Model Edge Percentage

The mathematical difference between our model's win probability and the market implied consensus. A positive edge identifies statistical divergence from market consensus.

Point Margin Differential

A statistical model projecting the final score differential between two opposing teams in a matchup.

Game Score Totals

A simulation-based projection of the combined total points or goals scored by both teams.
