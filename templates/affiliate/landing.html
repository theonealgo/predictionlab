The program at a glance

PredictionLab is a data-driven AI sports prediction product with daily picks across the NBA, NFL, MLB, NHL, soccer and more. As an affiliate, you share your unique referral link, and when someone you refer subscribes to a paid plan, you earn a commission on their qualifying subscription payments — including recurring renewals while they remain subscribed. Commissions are based on qualifying paid subscriptions and are subject to PredictionLab's affiliate terms.

Commissions are calculated on qualifying successful subscription payments. If a payment is refunded or charged back, the associated commission is reversed or adjusted. We do not offer lifetime commissions — you earn while the referred subscription remains active and qualifying.

Referral tracking & attribution

When a visitor arrives via your link (/?ref=YOURCODE), we record the click and store the attribution server-side against a first-party cookie — not just in the browser. The attribution follows the visitor as they browse and through the secure Stripe checkout. When they create an account and subscribe, the sale is attributed to you. The last valid affiliate click within the {{ cookie_days }}-day window wins; once a customer has converted, their attribution is locked so it can't be overwritten. Customers who already subscribed before clicking your link are not retro-attributed.

You'll find your referral link with one-click copy, performance reporting, a privacy-safe referrals table, a commissions ledger, payout tracking, and ready-to-use promotional copy.

FAQ

How much can I earn?

You earn {{ commission_pct }}% of qualifying paid subscription revenue from customers you refer, including recurring renewals while their subscription stays active and qualifying.

When do I get paid?

Commissions become payable after a {{ hold_days }}-day holding period (to account for refunds/chargebacks). Once your payable balance reaches {{ min_payout }}, you can request a payout from your dashboard.

How long is a referral tracked?

The referral attribution window is {{ cookie_days }} days from the last valid click. Attribution is stored server-side and survives checkout.

What happens on refunds or chargebacks?

The related commission is automatically reversed or adjusted so affiliates aren't credited for revenue that was returned.

Can I refer myself?

No. Self-referrals are detected and rejected, and abusive patterns are flagged for review.

Do you guarantee winnings or income?

No. PredictionLab is a sports prediction and research product. We make no guarantees of winnings or income — please promote honestly.
