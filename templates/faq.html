Frequently Asked Questions

How our AI predictions work, what the numbers mean, and what to expect from the models.

How do your AI sports predictions work?

Our predictions come from several independent AI models. Each model studies matchups, player performance and advanced team statistics and gives every game a win probability.

Every prediction is based on data, not opinion, and is updated as new information arrives.

How do your predictions compare with the market?

Market prices reflect public opinion as well as probability.

We show our own model probabilities next to the market's implied probability so you can see where the two disagree.

What does the probability percentage mean?

Each model gives a win probability for every game. A 60% prediction means the model expects that team to win about six times out of ten in games like this one.

Do your models agree on every game?

No. Each model uses a different method. We show every model's prediction and a consensus view so you can see where the models agree most.

What sports do you cover?

The NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, college football and basketball, the WNBA, CFL, soccer, tennis, UFC and golf.

Are your results tracked publicly?

Yes. Every prediction is graded in public, including wins, losses and performance over time.

Graded results appear on our results pages as games finish—nothing is edited after the fact and no losses are hidden.

Is this suitable for beginners?

Yes. Every card shows a plain win probability, and the free predictions are a good way to see how the models behave over time.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes. Weekly plans have a 3-day return/refund window, monthly plans have a 10-day window, and yearly plans have a 30-day window.

You can cancel anytime to stop future charges. Cancellation stops renewals; it is separate from a refund—refunds still apply only within those windows unless required by law. Full details: Refund & Return Policy.

Are your predictions always right?

No. Sports are unpredictable and no model is right every time. Our graded results show exactly how often each model has been right.

Who is Prediction Lab for?

Sports fans who want data-driven forecasts and a clear, honest record of how each model performs.

Expectations and responsible use

Predictions are probabilities, not certainties. Our tools are meant to inform your own judgment, not replace it.

If you are 21+ and choose to use them with licensed operators where legal, set limits and see our Responsible Gaming page.
