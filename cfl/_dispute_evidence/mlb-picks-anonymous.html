These picks are not meant to guarantee wins on a daily basis. Sports betting naturally includes variance, and even strong edges can result in short-term losses. The focus is on maintaining a structured approach and tracking performance over a larger sample size.

Users should approach these picks with proper bankroll management and realistic expectations.
